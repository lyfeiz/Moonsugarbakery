Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before you order. Still have a question? Reach out any time at hello@moonsugarbakery.com.

How far in advance should I order?

For custom and specialty work, please ask at least 1–2 weeks ahead when possible. Larger events and tiered cakes need more lead time. Last-minute? Ask anyway — sometimes it works out.

Do you offer vegan, gluten-free, and reduced-sugar options?

Yes, all three. Note it on your order. These are made with real technique so they taste like the original, not a compromise.

Can you ship my order?

Not currently. Under Texas cottage food rules, online orders are picked up in person or delivered locally in the El Paso area — no shipping.

Is there a delivery fee?

Delivery may carry a small upcharge depending on where you are in the El Paso area. Note delivery on your order and I'll confirm the details before you pay.

How do I pay?

After we confirm the details, you'll get a secure payment link. Custom cakes may need a deposit to reserve the date, with the balance due before pickup or delivery.

Are you certified?

Yes — ServeSafe certified, and baking professionally since 2017.

What about allergies?

Tell me in the order notes. I'll be honest about what I can safely accommodate in a home-kitchen environment.

What areas do you serve?

El Paso and the surrounding area, for local pickup and delivery.
