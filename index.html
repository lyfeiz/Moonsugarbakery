The Baker Behind the Bakery

Trained in French technique. Rooted in El Paso.

Moon Sugar Bakery started with a simple belief: a dessert should taste as beautiful as it looks. Since 2017, I've built my craft on French and European technique — cake decorating, chocolate tempering, bread baking, and plated desserts that treat every plate like a canvas.

My work has lived on El Paso menus, from reworking Café Central's Mango Colada pie to adding an original carrot cake to the permanent menu at West Texas. Right now I'm going deeper with chocolate, so the desserts keep getting more ambitious.
